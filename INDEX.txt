CONVENTIONS
-----------
- Binary by design: all per-layer analyses on A_l = 1[M_l>0]. c_l only enters Σ_l c_l·M_l.
- Reciprocity: pairs-based R_p = mutual_pairs / connected_pairs (main).
- Null: 10,000 directed degree-preserving edge-swap draws/layer (NetworkX algorithms.swap),
  Phipson–Smyth (M+1)/(N+1), BY-FDR default, BH side column.
- Confound pair: {CCAM, CCVM} reported separately.
- Model selection: k=1..8 × {full, diag, spherical} × {BIC, ICL, 10-fold CV, silhouette}.
